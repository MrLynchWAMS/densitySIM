How to use the Density Lab

  1. Drag an object onto the scale to read its mass. Keep only one object on the scale at a time.
  2. Drag the same object into the beaker. Volume = new water level − starting water level.
  3. Floating objects only displace part of their volume. Keep dragging them under the water to measure their full volume.
  4. Calculate density = mass ÷ volume. Enter your mass, volume, and density in the table.
  5. Compare your mystery density to the known materials, choose the material, and press Check.
Scoring:
Calculator tips: type directly or tap the buttons · Enter evaluates · Esc clears · click the scale or volume readout to insert its number · "Use in cell" puts the answer in the table cell you last clicked · Enter in a table cell jumps to the next one.
